Services

IT consulting across the full lifecycle

From a first strategy conversation to day-to-day support, Nolus Consulting covers the ground most businesses need an outside expert for.

IT strategy & advisory

Assessing where your IT stands today and mapping a realistic roadmap to where it needs to go.

Interim IT management

Stepping in as IT manager to lead the team and keep operations steady during a transition or gap.

Project & program management

Planning, running and reporting on IT projects and programs, from kickoff to go-live.

Product ownership & management

Owning the backlog and roadmap, translating business needs into what actually gets built.

Infrastructure & cloud

Designing, migrating and hardening the systems your business runs on — on-premise, cloud, or both.

Vendor & delivery management

Keeping external providers on track, on budget, and aligned with what the business actually needs.

Cybersecurity & compliance

Practical security hardening and compliance support, sized for how your organization actually operates.

Software & systems integration

Connecting the tools you already use, and helping you choose the ones you don't have yet.

Ongoing IT support

Ad-hoc or retained support once systems are live, so things keep running the way they should.
